How It Began

From a Passionate Crate Collection to India's Premier Vinyl Sanctuary

RetroSpin was born from a simple realization: too many timeless musical masterpieces were trapped on dusty, poorly handled records being discarded or sold by uneducated sellers who didn't understand the difference between surface dirt and groove wear.

What started as two passionate crate-diggers scouring estate sales across Chennai, Bangalore, Kochi, and international ports has blossomed into a full-fledged restoration lab and collector boutique. We’ve recovered and ultrasonically rejuvenated over 25,000 vintage records and rebuilt hundreds of classic turntables.

"When you drop a diamond stylus into a vinyl groove, you are physically bridging time. You are hearing the air in the recording room from 1959. That is irreplaceable."

Our Mission

To rescue, restore, and preserve recorded analog music heritage through uncompromising play-grading, medical-grade ultrasonic cleaning, and artisanal equipment maintenance—providing music lovers with the truest sound reproduction possible.

Our Vision

To inspire a new generation of listeners to pause the hurried noise of digital feeds and reclaim the immersive, communal ritual of listening to entire albums as artists originally intended—from Side A to Side B.

The Analog Argument

Why Vinyl in the Digital Age?

Vinyl is not nostalgia. It is an intentional, superior way of experiencing art.

Continuous Waveforms

Digital audio chops sound into 44,100 discrete slices per second. Vinyl is a continuous, unbroken physical curve that mirrors real-life acoustic air pressure waves.

Tangible Artwork

12-inch heavy gatefold cardboard jackets, textured paper, liner notes, and lyric sheets turn visual art into an intimate physical companion to the music.

Mindful Listening

You don't skip tracks every 20 seconds. You pull out the record, drop the needle, sit back on your armchair, and listen from opening chord to final groove.

Generational Value

Digital streaming services can delete albums overnight due to licensing disputes. A vinyl record is a physical heirloom that will spin 100 years from now.

The Protocol

Our 5-Stage Sourcing & Play-Grading Protocol

Every single record sold at RetroSpin passes through this rigorous chain.

1

Direct Analog Provenance Sourcing

We source directly from vetted private European, Japanese, and Indian audiophile estates, verifying matrix runout groove stampers and press plants (RTI, Pallas, QRP, EMI Hayes).

2

Dual-Spectrum Visual Inspection

Under 5000K daylight halogen and raking LED spotlights, we examine surfaces for hairline scratches, paper scuffs, spindle trails, and record warps.

3

Ultrasonic Cavitation Deep Cleaning

Submerged in an automated 40kHz ultrasonic bath with lab-grade surfactant to dislodge microscopic particulate before high-pressure vacuum drying.

4

Acoustic Play-Grading

Every pre-owned record is play-tested on high-resolution Audio-Technica and Ortofon cartridges through reference open-back headphones to grade surface noise objectively.

5

Archival Sleeving

We place every vinyl inside a brand-new anti-static HDPE inner sleeve and enclose the jacket in a heavy 3mil crystal-clear outer protective sleeve.

Acoustic Engineering

Chennai Flagship: Acoustic Architecture

Built from the ground up at 28 Khader Nawaz Khan Road to provide an uncompromised reference listening environment.

01 • Structural Isolation

Decoupled Floating Subfloors

Our listening room floors are structurally isolated using heavy Sylomer neoprene dampeners. This eliminates low-frequency rumbling from street traffic, allowing delicate 0.3mV moving-coil cartridges to track grooves without floor resonance.

Acoustic Floor < 15Hz Cutoff
02 • Teak 2D Diffusion

Angled Teak Diffusion Panels

Custom-crafted solid Burma teak 2D quadratic residue diffusers scatter acoustic energy between 450Hz and 6.5kHz. This eliminates harsh room flutter echoes while retaining the natural life, sparkle, and harmonic air of vocal recordings.

Diffusion Range 450Hz – 6.5kHz QRD
03 • Toroidal Power Grid

Toroidal Balanced Clean Power

A bespoke 5kVA medical-grade isolation transformer feeds purely symmetrical 230V mains power to our tube preamps, completely rejecting city power grid noise, EMI interference, and RF hum for a dead-silent noise floor.

Mains Filtering 5kVA Pure 230V
