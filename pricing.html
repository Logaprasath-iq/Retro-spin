Goldmine Standard

The Goldmine Vinyl Grading Scale

We adhere strictly to the internationally recognized Goldmine standard. Here is exactly what each grade means at RetroSpin.

Grade Vinyl Disc Condition Jacket & Cover Condition Sound Audition Quality
Mint (M) Factory sealed or absolutely pristine unplayed condition. Zero surface imperfections under microscope. Flawless jacket with sharp square corners, crisp spine text, and intact shrink-wrap where applicable. Complete acoustic silence on run-in and between tracks. Pure master fidelity.
Near Mint (NM) Nearly perfect. May have been played once or twice. Full original factory gloss; no scuffs or hairline marks. Very light shelf handling, but no seam splits, writing, or ring wear. Spine fully readable. Plays flawlessly with no audible ticks, clicks, or surface noise.
Very Good Plus (VG+) Shows slight signs of wear like minor paper scuffs or faint spindle marks that do not affect overall play. Minor corner dings, subtle edge wear, or light ring wear visible on dark jackets. No heavy tears. May have faint surface soft crackle in quiet passages or run-in, but music strongly overpowers it. Zero skips.
Very Good (VG) Surface scuffs and light scratches that can be felt with a soft fingernail. Still plenty of musical life. Moderate ring wear, minor seam splits, or tape residue. All text remains intact. Surface noise evident, especially in intros and quiet ballads, but record plays through without getting stuck.
