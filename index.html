Analog Mechanics

How a Vinyl Record Creates Sound

Zero digital compression. Just mechanical physics translating kinetic vibrations directly into pure human emotion.

Step 01 • Micro-Grooves

Microscopic Grooves

Analog sound waves are pressed as continuous physical modulations into vinyl walls at 45-degree angles—one wall carries the left stereo channel, the other carries the right.

Physics 45° Stereo Spiral
Step 02 • Diamond Stylus

Diamond Stylus

An ultra-fine elliptical or micro-line diamond tip tracks the groove contours at 1.8 grams of tracking force, vibrating in exact synchronization with original acoustic instruments.

Precision 1.8g Tracking Force
Step 03 • Induction Coil

Magnetic Induction

The vibrating cantilever moves microscopic magnets near pure oxygen-free copper coils, generating a minuscule electrical audio signal measured in sensitive millivolts (mV).

Electromagnetics 3.5mV Output Signal
Step 04 • RIAA EQ

Phono RIAA Equalization

The phono preamp reverses the RIAA recording curve to restore warm, tactile bass frequencies and boosts line voltage to feed the amplifier and drive loudspeaker cones.

Amplification RIAA Standard Curve
