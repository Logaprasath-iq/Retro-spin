Sound Science

The Pressing Shootout: Which Vinyl Sounds Best?

Not all vinyl records are pressed equal. Understanding the difference between mass commercial reissues and premium audiophile masterings changes how you collect.

Shop Auditioned Records

45 RPM 2xLP Deluxe Pressings

Ultimate Dynamic Range

By spreading music across four sides at 45 RPM, the groove travels past the stylus 35% faster. This yields dramatically lower inner-groove distortion, tighter bass transients, and shimmering high-frequency extension.

180g Virgin SuperVinyl (Tone Poet / QRP)

Zero Floor Noise

Pressed using non-recycled virgin vinyl pellets in dust-free cleanrooms. Heavy mass eliminates rotational platter slip and provides blacker, quieter acoustic backgrounds between song movements.

Abbey Road Half-Speed Mastering

Microscopic Precision

Lacquer cutting lathe runs at half speed (16 2/3 RPM for 33 1/3 playback), giving the cutting stylus double the electrical headroom to carve intricate wave envelopes without overheating.
